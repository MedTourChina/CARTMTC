How to Read This List

🎯

Target = the aim

CD19 CAR-Ts treat B-cell cancers (leukemias, lymphomas). BCMA CAR-Ts treat multiple myeloma. The target determines which disease a product can address.

📅

Indication = the approval

A product may be approved for one or more specific, often relapsed/refractory, conditions. Exact eligible lines of therapy differ by country and product.

🏢

Availability = the reality

Which product and indication are actually accessible to an international patient depends on the treating hospital and local approvals. We match you to the right center.

Important: Drug names, approvals and indications change. This page is educational. Your specialist will confirm the precise product, indication and eligibility for your case.

Cost & Access in China

China's CAR-T list prices are generally far below those in the US or Europe, and the total treatment episode is often a fraction of Western pricing. Growing local insurance and innovative payment schemes are improving access.

Why International Patients Come to China for CAR-T

Beyond cost, China offers high-volume cellular-therapy experience, short wait times for manufacturing at established centers, and internationally trained hematology teams. Many centers provide English-speaking coordination and dedicated international patient departments.
