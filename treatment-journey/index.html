Your CAR-T Treatment Journey

We coordinate every stage of your CAR-T experience in China — so you can focus on treatment and recovery.

Six Stages, One Coordinator

A dedicated international patient coordinator stays with your family from first contact to follow-up at home.

1

Inquiry & Record Review

Share your diagnosis and history. A hematology specialist reviews eligibility and the suitable CAR-T product(s) — free of charge.

2

Remote Consultation

A video or written second-opinion consultation with the specialist team to align on the plan before you travel.

3

Plan & Estimate

A transparent treatment plan and cost estimate, plus help with visa invitation letters, flights and accommodation.

4

Arrival & Evaluation

Airport pickup, hotel near the hospital, and admission for baseline tests and apheresis collection.

5

Treatment & Monitoring

Manufacturing, lymphodepletion, infusion and close inpatient monitoring by the CAR-T team with interpreter support.

6

Follow-up

Discharge summary in your language and structured remote follow-up coordinated with your home physician.

Planning Before You Fly
