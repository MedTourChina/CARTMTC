How We Choose the Right Hospital for You

The best center depends on your diagnosis, the CAR-T target you need (CD19 or BCMA), prior therapies and logistics. Our specialists shortlist 1–2 options and explain why.

1

Case Review

Our hematology team reviews your records to confirm CAR-T eligibility and the appropriate product/target.

2

Hospital Shortlist

We match you to centers that have the approved product and the experience for your specific disease.

3

Plan & Book

You receive a treatment plan, cost estimate and timeline, then we coordinate admission and travel.

Note: Specific hospital assignment is case-by-case and depends on product approvals and bed availability at the time of treatment. We confirm the exact center with you before any commitment.
