CAR-T Therapy Indications

Where CAR-T works today — and where it is heading. Most approved uses are in blood cancers, the first solid-tumour approval arrived in 2026, and autoimmune disease is now the fastest-moving field of all.

Approved Blood-Cancer Indications

CAR-T's most established successes are in B-cell malignancies (via CD19) and multiple myeloma (via BCMA).

🨄

Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (ALL)

CD19 CAR-T has transformed outcomes in relapsed/refractory B-ALL, including children and young adults who relapsed after standard therapy.

🩷

Non-Hodgkin & Large B-cell Lymphoma

The largest approved CAR-T field: diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL), follicular, mantle cell and transformed lymphomas after prior lines fail.

🩸

Multiple Myeloma

BCMA-targeted CAR-T offers deep responses for patients whose myeloma relapsed after multiple prior therapies.

By Target: CD19 vs. BCMA

💊
CD19

B-cell cancers

CD19 is present on most B cells and B-cell tumors. CD19 CAR-T treats B-ALL and a broad range of B-cell lymphomas. Because it also affects healthy B cells, patients may need immunoglobulin monitoring.

🩸
BCMA

Multiple myeloma

BCMA is highly expressed on myeloma cells. BCMA CAR-T has produced notable responses in heavily pre-treated myeloma, including patients who failed many prior lines.

The Solid-Tumor Frontier

CAR-T for solid cancers faces extra challenges — tumour heterogeneity, a hostile tumour microenvironment, and identifying truly tumour-specific targets. One target has cleared approval; a dozen more are in trials. This is where each one actually stands in 2026.

Only one is approved.
Gastric and junctional adenocarcinoma that tests CLDN18.2-positive and HER2-negative is the single solid-tumour indication with an approved CAR-T. Everything else in the table above is trial-only. A phase I response rate is not a licensed treatment — and the CD70 row is a reminder that early-phase solid-tumour CAR-T can be fatal.
How to read a response rate.
A 50% response in 12 patients and a 50% response in 120 patients are not the same claim. Ask for the denominator, the line of therapy, and whether the responses held at six months. We do this comparison for you when we review your records.

The Third Frontier: Autoimmune Disease

The most active area of CAR-T research outside cancer is not a tumour at all. It is lupus, systemic sclerosis and inflammatory myositis — diseases where the same engineered cell is used to reset a misdirected immune system rather than to kill a malignant one.

🫀

Systemic Lupus Erythematosus

The largest group treated so far. CD19 CAR-T depletes the B cells that produce autoantibodies, and drug-free remission has been reported in patients who had failed multiple lines of immunosuppression.

🫁

Systemic Sclerosis

Focused on the diffuse cutaneous form with internal-organ involvement. Early reports describe improvement in skin scores and, in some patients, in lung function.

🧠

Inflammatory Myositis

Including antisynthetase syndrome and immune-mediated necrotising myopathy, where standard immunosuppression has failed to control muscle inflammation.

Eighty percent sounds worse than it is.
Two thirds of that cohort had grade 1 only — fever and aches managed on a ward. One patient reached grade 3. Read the whole distribution, not the headline number.
Where China sits.
No CAR-T product is approved in China for any autoimmune indication as of November 2026. Access is through clinical trials and, at a small number of centres, physician-initiated programmes. If a clinic offers you “approved” CAR-T for lupus in China, that claim needs checking.

Is CAR-T Right for You? Eligibility Basics

The same three questions apply whether the diagnosis is a lymphoma, a gastric cancer or lupus.

📊

Disease & line of therapy

The diagnosis and how many prior treatments you have had determine which CAR-T product and indication may apply.

🩹

Organ function

Adequate heart, lung, liver and kidney function, and ability to tolerate lymphodepletion, are assessed.

👶

Disease burden & fitness

Controllable disease burden and overall fitness for the collection, manufacturing and monitoring process are reviewed.
